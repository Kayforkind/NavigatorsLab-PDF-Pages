📖 Detailed Step-by-Step User Guide

Step 1: Drop One or More PDFs to Load Page Thumbnails · [PDF Dropzone (#dz)]Drag one or more `.pdf` files onto `#dz`. Dropping multiple PDFs appends all of their pages into one unified thumbnail grid.✓ Result: `pdf.js` renders a live visual thumbnail card (`.pp-cell`) for every page.
Step 2: Drag Thumbnails to Reorder, Rotate 90°, or Delete · [Thumbnail Grid (#pages)]In `#pages`, drag and drop `.pp-cell` cards to reorder them, click **↻** on a card to rotate that page 90°, or click **× (`.pp-x`)** to remove a page.✓ Result: Page sequence and orientations update visually in real time.
Step 3: Select Specific Pages or Insert Blank Separator Pages · [Page Checkboxes & Insert Blank (#blank)]Toggle the checkbox on individual `.pp-cell` cards to pick specific pages for extraction, or click **Insert blank (`#blank`)** to add a clean separator page.✓ Result: Custom page selection or blank separator pages added to the layout.
Step 4: Rebuild Full PDF or Extract Selected Pages · [Rebuild (#rebuild) & Extract (#extract)]Click **Rebuild PDF (`#rebuild`)** to download the full reordered/merged document, or **Extract selected (`#extract`)** to export only the checked pages.✓ Result: Lossless vector page copy built via `pdf-lib` and downloaded locally.

🎛️ UI Controls & Shortcuts Reference

  • Thumbnail Grid (`#pages` / `.pp-cell`) — Drag-and-drop visual cards for every page across one or more loaded PDFs.
  • Per-Page Rotate (`↻`) & Delete (`.pp-x`) — Rotate individual pages in 90° increments or delete unwanted cover/blank pages.
  • Insert Blank (`#blank`) — Inserts a blank separator page into the document sequence.
  • Rebuild (`#rebuild`) & Extract (`#extract`) — Exports either the entire modified deck or only the checked pages as a new PDF.

💡 Pro Tips & Real-World Workflows

  • To merge two or three separate PDFs into one packet, simply drop all of them onto `#dz` one after another, drag the thumbnails into order, and click **Rebuild PDF**.
  • Because `pdf-lib` copies page dictionary nodes directly without rasterizing, vector text and embedded fonts stay 100% sharp and searchable.
  • Need deeper in-place text editing, redaction, or OCR on those pages? Open **PDF Studio (`/pdf-studio/`)**.
